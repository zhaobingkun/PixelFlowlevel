- 2026-07-27: Clarified the relationship between AdSense invalid traffic and content/copyright risk using current Google Publisher/AdSense policy documentation plus local page inspection. Google treats invalid traffic (automated or repeated impressions/clicks, accidental clicks, publisher-induced clicks, and other artificial activity) separately from copyright and replicated-content policy violations. Therefore the account-level `invalid traffic concerns` label should not be interpreted as a hidden copyright finding. PixelFlow nevertheless has an independent inventory-value risk: thousands of level pages embed YouTube videos and use structurally similar, generic walkthrough copy. Google specifically flags pages dedicated to embedded third-party media without substantial original commentary, curation, or added value. Official YouTube embedding is not by itself proof of copyright infringement, and the risk is lower for videos owned or licensed by the publisher, but each monetized level page should contain demonstrably level-specific analysis rather than interchangeable route advice. As of the 2026-07-24 Policy Center review, the confirmed enforcement was invalid traffic; no separate copyright or replicated-content finding had been recorded.
